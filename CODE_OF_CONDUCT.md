# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity or expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, sexual identity
or orientation, or other protected status.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, precise, and constructive.
- Critique ideas, code, methods, and evidence rather than people.
- State scientific uncertainty and assumptions honestly.
- Accept correction and take responsibility for mistakes.
- Protect private, confidential, and unpublished research data.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory conduct, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, doxxing, and sustained disruption are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to project maintainers using the private
contact route described in `SECURITY.md`; do not include sensitive personal
details in a public issue. Maintainers will review reports fairly, protect
confidentiality as practical, and may edit or remove contributions, warn,
temporarily restrict, or permanently ban participants based on severity and
pattern. Anyone with a conflict of interest must recuse themselves.

This policy applies in project spaces and when an individual is officially
representing the project elsewhere.
